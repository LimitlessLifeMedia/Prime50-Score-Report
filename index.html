Your PRIME50 report

Each answered item contributes 0–4 points, with equal weighting. A complete total is points ÷ 60 × 100; each complete category is points ÷ 20 × 100. Higher reflects greater reported ease or consistency, not biological age or a diagnosis. Habit scores are reflection prompts, not recommended training frequencies. “Not sure” answers are never treated as zero.
